03 / THE PART WE DON'T LIKE TO ADMIT

Good and evil
aren't always obvious.

Sometimes the hardest part isn't choosing a side. It's questioning why you chose it.

“

A hero would sacrifice you for the world.
A villain would sacrifice the world for you.

”

THE HERO'S QUESTION

How many can you save?

Doing what helps the most people can still hurt someone you care about. A good intention doesn't make every choice painless.

THE VILLAIN'S QUESTION

Who gets to matter?

Loving one person deeply doesn't give anyone the right to decide that everyone else is disposable.

THE HUMAN QUESTION

Can you love someone
without making everyone else expendable?

Protecting one person can feel natural. But care becomes dangerous when it turns into a reason to ignore the lives and dignity of everyone outside your circle.
